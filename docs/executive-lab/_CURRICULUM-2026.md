## FULL MODULE DETAIL (Jun–Dec — the part that was previously wrong)

### JUN — Build Your Board of Advisors (Lane: Relationships) ✅ built
Build the personal board that carries your growth; tell apart advisors, mentors, sponsors. Map current relationships, name gaps, plan a balanced board (mentors, sponsors, peers, connectors).
**Key distinction:** Mentors advise you (talk WITH you) | Sponsors advocate for you (talk ABOUT you) — you need both.
**Anchor tools (per Wendy):** Board of Advisors Mapping Worksheet · Sponsor vs. Mentor Guide · Relationship Gap Analysis · Reciprocity Playbook · Outreach & Ask Scripts
**WOC focus:** WOC are often over-mentored and under-sponsored — rich in advice, short on advocacy.
> NOTE: what we shipped in module-6 covers this well (Advisor Map, Sorter, Outreach & Ask, Nurture Tracker). Wendy's tool list also names a "Reciprocity Playbook" + "Sponsor vs. Mentor Guide" — optional additions if she wants exact-match.

### JUL–AUG — Summer Integration (Relationships + Sustainability, self-paced) — WORKSHEETS ONLY
NO live module. Participants get self-paced worksheets.
**Handouts:** Board of Advisors Deep Dive (extends June) · Sponsor vs. Mentor Guide · Energy Reset & the Cultural Tax Workbook (Energy Audit, Recovery Ritual Planner, Cultural Tax Inventory) · Mid-Year Reflection Workbook · Networking for Introverts · The Reciprocity Playbook · Summer Reading List
**Community:** accountability pairings, weekly prompts, Win of the Week, optional book club.

### SEP — Executive Storytelling: Master Your Narrative (Lane: Value)
Turn wins into compelling stories. Feb = strategy (who to reach); Sep = technique (how to say it). Story structures (STAR, CAR), a 2-min Signature Story, a portfolio of context-specific stories.
**Key distinction:** Feb = STRATEGY (who/when/where) | Sep = TECHNIQUE (how to say it).
**Anchor tools:** Story Structure Templates (STAR, CAR, CARL) · Signature Story Worksheet · Wins-to-Stories Converter · Delivery Self-Assessment Checklist
**WOC focus:** owning your narrative without minimizing accomplishments or centering others.

### OCT — Inclusive Leadership: Lead Authentically & Build Your Coalition (Lane: Leadership + Culture)
Lead inclusive teams while staying authentic. Leading as "the only," building coalition power, creating psychological safety, mentoring next gen without burnout.
**Anchor tools:** Leadership Identity Worksheet · Inclusive Leadership Self-Assessment · Coalition Mapping Exercise · Difficult Conversation Prep Template · Support Ecosystem Blueprint
**WOC focus:** code-switching fatigue; authentic voice; collective/coalition power; leading while "other."

### NOV — Evidence-Based Confidence: From Self-Doubt to Self-Trust (Lane: Sustainability)
Reframe internal narratives; build self-trust on evidence, not affirmations. Cognitive work. Build an Evidence File of proof points; evidence-based affirmations.
**Key distinction:** Apr = moments | Summer = energy | Nov = MINDSET.
**Anchor tools:** Imposter Syndrome Self-Assessment · Evidence File Template · Reframing Worksheet · Daily Confidence Practice Guide · Evidence-Based Affirmations Builder
**WOC focus:** distinguishing imposter syndrome from systemic oppression — internal doubt vs. real barriers.

### DEC — Reclaim Your Time: Boundaries, Decisions & Your Personal Operating System (Lane: Sustainability)
Personal operating system: boundaries, priorities, decision frameworks. Choices/systems, not energy. Yes/No Decision Framework, boundary scripts, 90-Day Sustainability Plan.
**Key distinction:** Summer = ENERGY | Dec = DECISIONS.
**Anchor tools:** Boundaries Workbook · Yes/No Decision Framework · Boundary Scripts Library · Calendar Audit Template · 90-Day Sustainability Plan · Board of Advisors Maintenance Tracker
**WOC focus:** permission to protect time when socialized to accommodate.
**Program close:** celebrate the year's wins, reinforce tools, set intentions; consider a recognition ceremony.
